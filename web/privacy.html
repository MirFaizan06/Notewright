Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Your audio stays on your device

Notewright converts recordings to MIDI entirely on your phone or in your browser. Recordings, the notes found in them and the MIDI files you create are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We do not run accounts, analytics or tracking of our own. We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

Advertising

Notewright is free and supported by ads. In the Android app the ads are provided by Google AdMob and by advertising partners that Google works with. The website version of Notewright currently shows no ads; if that changes, this page will be updated first. To show and measure ads, these providers can process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, browser or device information and how you interact with ads. They do this under their own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions you are asked for consent before personalised ads are used. You can change that choice at any time from About → Ad privacy choices in the Android app. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device settings.

Update checks

The app may ask GitHub for the latest release number so it can tell you about updates. That request goes to GitHub and is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Notewright is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by local law) and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
