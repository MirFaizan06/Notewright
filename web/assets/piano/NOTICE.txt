Salamander Grand Piano V2 (Yamaha C5)
Recorded and edited by Alexander Holm.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

These 30 MP3 files (every third key from A0 to C8, one velocity layer) are the
compact set distributed in the Tone.js audio repository
(https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander). They are used here
unmodified, only to play back the MIDI preview inside Notewright.
The original readme is kept in SOURCE-README.txt.
